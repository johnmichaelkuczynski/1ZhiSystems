=========================================================
NEW LOGIC FOR SUB-FUNCTION:
SCHEMA EQUIVALENCE: MINIMAL OBSTRUCTION REPORT (T₁, T₂)
=========================================================

TASK:
Given two theories T₁ and T₂, output a **single, minimal, formal
obstruction** explaining why they FAIL schema-equivalence under
any of the following mapping types:

1. Direct Vocabulary Mapping  
2. Arity-Preserving Mapping  
3. Structural Role Mapping  

This sub-function NEVER declares equivalence.
Its sole purpose is to identify the FIRST point of failure.

=========================================================
ORDER OF TESTING
=========================================================

The sub-function must test obstacles in this EXACT sequence:

1. **Cardinality Mismatch**  
   |Lang(T₁)| ≠ |Lang(T₂)|

2. **Arity Mismatch**  
   ∃i such that arity(Pi) ≠ any arity in T₂

3. **No Possible Arity-Preserving Bijection**  
   Multisets of arities differ.

4. **Role-Profile Mismatch**  
   Even with matching arities, no bijection aligns role profiles.

5. **Axiom Mismatch Under Best Candidate Mapping**  
   Even with a candidate bijection, rewritten axioms of T₁ fail to match T₂.

Upon encountering the FIRST failing condition, STOP and report ONLY that obstruction.

=========================================================
MINIMAL OBSTRUCTION FORMAT
=========================================================

Each possible failure has a fixed, canonical message:

1. CARDINALITY:
   "Obstruction: Different number of predicate symbols."

2. ARITY MISMATCH:
   "Obstruction: Predicate arities do not match across theories."

3. ARITY MULTISET FAILURE:
   "Obstruction: No arity-preserving bijection exists."

4. ROLE PROFILE FAILURE:
   "Obstruction: No predicate-role mapping preserves structural profiles."

5. AXIOM FAILURE:
   "Obstruction: Axioms do not match under any arity-preserving renaming."

Exactly ONE of the above must be output.
