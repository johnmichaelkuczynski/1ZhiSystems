Language:
Organism(x), Parent(x,y), Trait(x,t), Survives(x)

Axioms:

∀x (Organism(x) → ∃y Parent(x,y))

∀x∀y (Parent(x,y) → Organism(x) ∧ Organism(y))

∀x∀t ((Trait(x,t) ∧ Parent(x,y)) → ∃t' Trait(y,t'))

∀x∀y ((Parent(x,y) ∧ Parent(x,z)) → y = z)

∀x ((∃t Trait(x,t)) → (Survives(x) ∨ ¬Survives(x)))

∃x∃y (Trait(x,t) ∧ ¬Trait(y,t))

What it captures:
Reproduction, inheritance with variation, survival without teleology.
